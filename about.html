    Knitting Q

    I started knitting in 2024, and ever since, I've wanted to create and design my own knitwear. Coming from a background in mathematics and technology, I bring a slightly different perspective to the craft — I tech edit my own designs, combining creativity with a bit of problem-solving along the way.

    I don't have a particular theme or style that defines my designs. Instead, I enjoy designing whatever comes to mind and drawing inspiration from the things around me. One day, that might be a simple, timeless staple sweater; the next, something colourful, playful. I like letting ideas take shape naturally and seeing where they lead.

    This is my little corner to share my designs, patterns, and knitting journey. For the latest updates, new designs, and behind-the-scenes glimpses, follow me on Instagram @knitting__q or feel free to get in touch by email. I'd love to hear from you!
